## English

**Name** (15 / 30): College Tracker

**Subtitle** (23 / 30): HK & Japan applications

**Keywords** (84 / 100): `jupas,eju,university,college,hongkong,japan,deadline,admissions,reminder,dse,pathway`

**Description**

College Tracker is for students applying to universities in Hong Kong and Japan.

Search a bundled catalog of Hong Kong and Japanese universities, save the schools you are considering, and keep deadlines, documents, and notes on this iPhone. Pathways describe typical routes: JUPAS and non-JUPAS in Hong Kong, and EJU, the Common Test, comprehensive selection (AO), and school recommendation in Japan. They are catalog guidance, not official deadlines. Check the current JUPAS schedule, the university’s own guide, or the 募集要項.

When you add a deadline, the app can remind you on this device at 9:00 — 30 days before, one week before, one day before, and on the day. These are ordinary local notifications, not Critical Alerts.

Your list stays on this iPhone. There is no College Tracker account. If you later turn on iCloud for the app, your data can sync through your private iCloud database. Counselor sharing is not in this version.

The app is in English, Japanese, and Traditional Chinese.

**What’s New**

First release for students applying in Hong Kong and Japan. Save schools from the catalog, track JUPAS, non-JUPAS, EJU, and other deadlines, and get local reminders at 9:00.

**Promotional text** (optional, 103 / 170): Track JUPAS, non-JUPAS, and EJU deadlines for Hong Kong and Japan. Local reminders at 9:00. No account.

**Category:** Education. **Secondary:** Productivity.
